BẰNG CHỨNG CẤU HÌNH ĐANG CHẠY — LAB 1
Nguồn: docker compose exec waf nginx -T; /etc/modsecurity.d/setup.conf

Nginx:
proxy_pass http://web:3000;
modsecurity on;
modsecurity_rules_file /etc/modsecurity.d/setup.conf;

ModSecurity/CRS:
SecRuleEngine On
Include /etc/modsecurity.d/owasp-crs/crs-setup.conf
Include /etc/modsecurity.d/owasp-crs/rules/*.conf

Compose:
BACKEND=http://web:3000
MODSEC_RULE_ENGINE=On
BLOCKING_PARANOIA=1
